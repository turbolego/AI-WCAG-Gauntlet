What is WCAG 2.2 Level AAA?

WCAG 2.2 Level AAA represents the highest level of web accessibility conformance. It includes all Level A and AA criteria plus additional requirements such as enhanced color contrast (7:1 ratio), sign language interpretation, and extended audio descriptions.
