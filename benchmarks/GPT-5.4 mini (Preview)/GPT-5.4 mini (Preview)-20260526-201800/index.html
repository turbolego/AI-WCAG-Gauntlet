    Semantic structure

    Why this page exists

    It gives the benchmark a rich landmark tree and enough tag diversity to exercise validation, accessibility, and content rendering.

    Strong text, emphasis, bold, italic, underline, struck, small print, marked detail, and inline span styling all appear in one paragraph.

    W3C, definition, WCAG 2.2, Perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust, and one hundred ten provide semantic examples.

    Keyboard cues: Tab to move focus, Shift + Tab to move back, and OK for a sample response. Math-like notation uses score, sup, and sub.

    Accessible content should be understandable without forcing a user to infer structure from presentation.
